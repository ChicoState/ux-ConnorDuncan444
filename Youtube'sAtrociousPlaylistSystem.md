# Youtube's Atrocious Playlist System
## Usability Journal Entry 2

Ever since I was young I spent a unhealthy amount of time on Youtube. I would get home from school then spend the entire rest of the day in front of my computer watching video game lets plays, comedy skits, animations, and music. Even when I wasn't actively watching a Youtube video I probably had some music running in the background on Youtube. Even as I write this I'm using Youtube for music. I've been using Youtube for over a decade which is why I am so dismayed to say that the modern Youtube playlist system is in a miserable state. It is riddled with blatant bugs, malicious design, and obnoxious user interface decisions that routinely infuriate me.
