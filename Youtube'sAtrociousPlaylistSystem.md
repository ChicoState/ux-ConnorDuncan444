# Youtube's Atrocious Playlist System
## Usability Journal Entry 2

Ever since I was young I spent an unhealthy amount of time on Youtube. I would get home from school then spend the entire rest of the day in front of my computer watching video game lets plays, comedy skits, animations, and music. Even when I wasn't actively watching a Youtube video I probably had some music running in the background on Youtube. Even as I write this I'm using Youtube for music. I've been using Youtube for over a decade which is why I am so dismayed to say that the modern Youtube playlist system is in a miserable state. It is riddled with blatant bugs, malicious design, and obnoxious user interface decisions that routinely infuriate me.
Many of the issues present here aren't a big deal when you are watching a small playlist. Woe be upon me then that my go to choice for music recently has been a collaborative playlist my friends put together called the car playlist which contains over 600 songs.

### A Highly Abbreviated List of my Issues with Youtube Playlists:

**Routine Failure to Load Videos**
  - I've used Youtube for a long time which gives me the misfortune of remembering an era in which Youtube videos would just load quickly and efficiently. Now videos take an excessive amount of time load assuming they even load at all. Having to wait 5-10 seconds for a video to load wouldn't be an issue if I were just watching a few videos, but I watch dozens of videos every day. Furthermore, sometimes the videos simply don't load. I'm uncertain if this is intentional or just a glitch but in order to get a playlist to actually load the next video after the previous one is finished you have to trigger the "loop playlist" setting. If all I had to do to fix it was hit the setting I wouldn't be so irritated, but for some reason the setting resets every time you skip a video in the playlist which I often do because I'm listening to music. This causes videos to routinely fail to load.

**Shuffle Is A Lie!**
  - The shuffle feature should be ridiculously simple. Randomly generate a number between one and the number of videos in the playlist then load the video at that index. This is too difficult for Google to do apparently. Youtube's shuffle isn't random, it is instead based on some behind the scene algorithm that tries to play the videos it thinks you want instead of just shuffling the damn playlist. This causes Youtube playlists to ignore several videos in my playlist while repeatedly showing me a few videos. Furthermore, sometimes the shuffle will just break entirely. A routine occurrence  is a glitch where Youtube will loop 3-6 songs. This forces me to stop what I'm doing to manually break the loop.

### Stop Recommending Me Shit
  - The music playlist my friends and I have made, which we call the car playlist, as of writing this, has 620 songs on it. However, not all 620 songs are created equal; sometimes when I listen to this playlist I want to start at a specific point. Often that point is at the bottom of the playlist where the songs I recently added are. Navigating to the bottom of such a long playlist is very inconvenient. Fortunately, if you middle mouse click and drag down your computer will automatically scroll to the bottom of the page. This USED to be an effective method to quickly get to the bottom of the playlist with minimal effort. This was too nice so Youtube decided to change this by adding multiple recommended bars to the bottom of the playlist. Now when you try to use this strategy the playlist will stop loading part way through because it thinks you are looking at their stupid recommended bars! I have to manually scroll back up so that more of the playlist will load before I can keep scrolling down.

### These issues are hurting the efficiency, effectiveness, and satisfaction of Youtube's playlist System.
All of these as one off issues would be annoying at most, but the combination of multiple bugs, bad design choices, and shameless attempts to overcharge the dopamine receptors of the users have produced a product that is clunky and irritating to use. The central focus of usability can be summarized by three values, efficiency, effectiveness, and satisfaction. I feel that Youtube's playlist system has failings in all three areas.

  -Efficiency:
Efficiency refers to how quickly the user can complete their goals within the program. Slow load times on both videos and the playlist display hurt the efficiency of the app. The presence of shortcuts to quickly traverse a playlist would help with efficiency; their absence is frustrating because of how obvious of a solution it is.

  -Effectiveness
Effectiveness refers to the user's ability to successfully complete their goals within the program. Videos failing to load are a clear issue of effectiveness for a video hosting platform. I see no solution for this besides fixing the issue.

  -Satisfaction
Satisfaction refers to the feelings the user experiences when using the program. In case you cannot tell by my tone within this entry I am not satisfied. It's especially bad because many of these issues did not used to exist within Youtube. Because I have been using this program for so long I have heightened expectations for what it should be able to do and in my opinion the app is in a worse state now than it was when I was younger.

I find these issues to be inexcusable for a multi-billion dollar company that started with an already functional product and I would consider this to be a failure in usability.
